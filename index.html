Welcome to JSCA SK

Jordanian Swedish Chess Club — Promoting Chess Excellence in Sweden

JSCA SK is an active chess association in Sweden, founded with a clear mission: to promote the love of chess at every level — from curious beginners to competitive masters.

We organise tournaments, training camps and festivals in collaboration with the Stockholm Chess Federation and international academies, including a formal partnership with the prestigious Abu Dhabi Chess Academy.

♞ Jönköping Chess Festival 2026 — 3rd Edition

Dates: July 25 – August 1, 2026  |  Location: Scandic Elmia Hotel, Jönköping  |  Organiser: JSCA TEAM  |  Registration Deadline: July 14, 2026

Hosted by the Jordanian Swedish Chess Academy, the festival features multiple tournaments across all skill levels — from FIDE-rated standard events to blitz, bullet, and family tournaments.

Tournament Categories

  • Standard Open — FIDE-Swiss format, open to all. Free entry for GMs, WGMs, IMs & WIMs.
  • Under 1800 — 6-round FIDE-Swiss for players rated below 1800 ELO.
  • Rapid Open & Rapid Women — Time control: 10 min + 3 sec/move.
  • Blitz & Bullet Open — Fast-paced action with deep prize pools.
  • Family & Amateur Tournaments — Fun, unrated events for parent-child or sibling teams.

Fees & Registration

Standard entry fees range from 444 SEK to 1,111 SEK depending on age, title, and registration date. Pre-register before June 25 to receive an early-bird discount. Register online via the Swedish Chess Federation (Sveriges Schackförbund).
